My Websites

Tab to SheetMusic

Turn any Songsterr tab into printable sheet music: standard notation, tablature, or both. Listen along, practice with a loop and a speed trainer, edit the notes, and download as Guitar Pro or MIDI.

Open the website » Source code

Pigeon PalGames

A friendly coach for GamePigeon games. It shows the best move, finds every word in Word Hunt and Anagrams, tells you where the enemy ships are hiding, and lets you practice against a strong computer. Works offline once loaded.

Play it » Source code

RemapWindows

A small key remapper for Windows, built on AutoHotkey v2. Remaps live in profiles that only switch on while one of their programs is the window you're in. Comes with a fix for playing Hotline Miami on a laptop.
