my websites

tab to sheetmusic

turn any songsterr tab into sheet music you can print: regular notation, tab, or both. you can also listen along, loop the tricky parts, slow things down, and fix notes yourself.

open it » source code

pigeon palgames

a little helper for gamepigeon games. it shows you the best move, finds every word in word hunt and anagrams, figures out where the ships are hiding in sea battle, and lets you practice against a really good bot. works offline too.

play it » source code

remapwindows

a small key remapper for windows, made with autohotkey. you set up profiles that only kick in while a certain program is open. comes with a fix for playing hotline miami on a laptop.
