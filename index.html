my project

tab to sheet

give it a songsterr link (or search for a song) and it turns the tab into sheet music you can print. it plays the song back too, and you can loop a part or slow it down to practice. it can also save the song as a guitar pro or midi file.

remap

remaps keys on windows, but only while a certain program is open, so nothing else gets messed up. it runs on autohotkey v2. there's a hotline miami profile in it that makes e a right click, which is way easier on a laptop.
